Level Up U7Buy

U7Buy, a marketplace for game items, coins, accounts and top-ups: Fortnite, FC 26, Roblox and more. A full redesign from the category page to the seller cabinet, desktop and mobile. Four milestones, hundreds of frames, every screen reworked, in an 8-page comic.

The problem

  • An existing product: users already have habits, a catalogue structure and commerce flows.
  • A redesign could not be just a new paint job. It had to balance a modern look with what users rely on.
