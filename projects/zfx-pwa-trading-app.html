Trade From the Browser

A forex and crypto trading app built as a PWA: it installs from the browser, no app store. Designed end to end: login, markets, deal ticket, portfolio and web view, in one dark purple system for phone and desktop. An 11-page comic.

Start reading Open PDF (8 MB)

The problem

  • ZFX had to reach traders in many countries, but App Store and Google Play access could be limited in some regions.
  • The answer: an experimental PWA, the core of a trading platform right in the browser, no store install.

My role

Senior Product Designer · ZEAL / ZFX

Experimental PWA · the first step of the ecosystem

  1. 1

    Own the UX

    User experience, interface structure and visual decisions.

  2. 2

    Fit the small screen

    Keep full trading functionality within the limits of mobile web.

  3. 3

    Less complexity

    Clear for beginners, fast access to tools for pros.

  4. 4

    Into a new app

    After launch the PWA learnings shaped a brand-new ZFX mobile app.

  5. 5

    Then the web

    The same product logic grew into a desktop trading platform.

Key valueLess complexity, no lost features: clear for beginners, fast for pros.
