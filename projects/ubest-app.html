Hire Like a Match

uBest, an AI-powered recruitment platform for the UAE, designed from zero as a startup. 800 applications per vacancy, recruiters picking by luck: so both sides fill in a profile and the algorithm matches them, like a dating app. Market research, matching logic, the candidate app and the company platform, in a 10-page comic.

Start reading Open PDF (3 MB)

The problem

  • A new market for us: hiring in the UAE, with talent from the UAE, CIS and Europe. We had to understand how it works before drawing anything.
  • 800 applications per vacancy, recruiters picking by luck. The idea: match people and jobs like a dating app, once both fill in a profile.
