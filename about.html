What product experience do I bring?

I have worked across fintech, crypto trading, telecom, e-commerce, travel, AI products and SaaS. My experience covers product strategy, UX research, user flows, high-fidelity UI, design systems, dashboards, onboarding, KYC, growth funnels and AI-assisted product design.

How did I work with financial and trading products?

At ZEAL / ZFX, I led UX/UI design for trading, onboarding, KYC and transaction flows across web, mobile and PWA products. One of the key outcomes was reducing KYC rejection reasons from 100+ to 17 and simplifying complex operational workflows.

At 3Commas, I designed crypto trading dashboards, investor workflows and fintech interfaces for complex trading scenarios.

What was my experience with large digital companies?

At Wildberries, I worked on marketplace interfaces, seller and customer journeys, e-commerce flows, product pages and operational workflows.

At Yandex, I designed web and mobile product interfaces, internal tools, user flows, prototypes and product design deliverables for complex digital scenarios.

What do I do as a Lead Product Designer?

I connect product thinking with hands-on execution: research, UX logic, interface design, design systems, growth experiments and collaboration with product, engineering and analytics teams. I’m comfortable owning product surfaces from discovery to delivery and working in international teams.
